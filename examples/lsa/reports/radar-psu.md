Non-economic constraints to settle before choosing:

- Repair onboard (card swap): capacitor bank discharge hazard: safe onboard repair not yet confirmed (open question on EC-DEMO-001)
- Repair onboard (card swap): needs a technician qualified on the new unit in every crew
- Repair at OEM: export and controlled-goods paperwork for each shipment
